How a turnover works

  1. Walkthrough and scope

    We walk the unit, photograph its condition, and list every item that needs work.

  2. Fixed per-unit quote

    You get one price for the full scope, so there are no surprise line items after the work starts.

  3. Work scheduled around your vacancy

    We plan the job around move-out and your next showing, and keep you updated as it moves along.

  4. Photo punch-list sign-off

    Every item on the scope is checked and photographed before we call the unit done.

FAQ

Common questions.

What areas do you serve?
We serve the greater Indianapolis area, including Indianapolis, Brownsburg, Avon, Plainfield, Zionsville, Carmel, Fishers, Westfield, Noblesville, Greenwood, and nearby towns in Marion, Hendricks, Boone, Hamilton, Johnson, Morgan, and Hancock counties.
Do you work with property management companies and landlords?
Yes. We handle single units and ongoing turnover work for property managers, investors, and individual landlords across greater Indianapolis, with per-unit pricing and photo documentation on every job.
Can you handle the whole turnover, or only part of it?
The whole turnover. Paint, flooring, drywall, plumbing and electrical repairs, HVAC service, appliances, cleaning, junk removal, and exterior cleanup are all covered under one per-unit price, with one point of contact from walkthrough to sign-off.
How long does a turnover take?
It depends on the scope. A paint-and-clean unit moves faster than one that needs new flooring or repairs. You get a timeline with your quote, planned around the move-out date and your next showing.
